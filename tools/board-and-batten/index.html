What is Field Mode?

Field Mode turns your phone or tablet into a giant step-by-step marking guide. Start at the same end you measured from, place a mark at the number on screen, then tap Next. It shows one mark at a time so you do not have to read a crowded list while holding a tape measure.

1 · Hook your tape at the starting edge
2 · Mark the displayed measurement
3 · Tap Next
