How MeasureMarks chooses the layout

A centered tile layout can be centered either on the middle of a tile or on the middle of a grout joint. Both are symmetrical, but they can produce very different border cuts. MeasureMarks calculates both and favors the layout with the larger usable edge cut while checking your preferred minimum.
