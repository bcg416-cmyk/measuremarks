ABOUT

Built for the part between measuring and building.

MeasureMarks is an independent collection of project-smart layout tools designed to turn real dimensions into usable measurements, drawings, and printable job sheets.

Why MeasureMarks exists

Many DIY calculators stop at a number. MeasureMarks is built around the way a project actually gets laid out: measure the real space, enter the dimensions, see the arrangement, mark the locations, and take a clear plan to the work area.

What makes the tools different

Each project-specific calculator is designed around the details of the job. Fence tools account for picket or post widths and spacing limits. Tile tools compare balanced layouts. Recessed-light tools produce two-dimensional coordinates. Cabinet hardware tools map drill locations. Picture-hanging tools account for frame sizes and hanger positions.

Whenever practical, the result includes a scaled visual blueprint, dimension callouts, a mark list, a printable job sheet, and Field Mode for step-by-step layout work.

Our approach

The goal is straightforward: useful tools, clear assumptions, transparent measurements, and enough visual context that you can sanity-check the math before using it on a project.

Independence and advertising

MeasureMarks may be supported by advertising. Advertising does not determine calculator results or project recommendations. Sponsored or advertising areas are kept separate from the calculator inputs and core results.
